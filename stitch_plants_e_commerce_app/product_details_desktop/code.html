eco Detailed Seasonal Watering & Feeding Routine expand_more

Spring & Summer (Vigorous Growth): Water thoroughly when top 50% of the soil profile dries out. Feed every 2–3 weeks with balanced organic liquid plant food. Leaves will begin uncurling dramatic split fenestrations.

Fall & Winter (Dormancy): Reduce watering cadence by 30-40%. Keep away from heating vents or frosty windowpanes. Mist leaves or wipe with a damp microfiber cloth to remove dust.

inventory Unboxing & Acclimation Instructions expand_more

Your specimen arrives securely nestled inside a bespoke reinforced biodegradable cylinder. Gently remove the protective breathable paper sleeve. Check the moisture gauge stick inserted into the root ball. Do NOT repot for the first 21 days while your Monstera adjusts to your home's unique light and humidity conditions.
